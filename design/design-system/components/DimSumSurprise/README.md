# DimSumSurprise

An occasional dim sum card with a real catalogue photo, credit, explicit loading and unavailable states, and an off switch. Hidden entirely in the school mode.

- **Material 3 primitive:** Card
- **Covers:** `dim-sum`, `dim-sum-photo-source`, `school-mode`

## Provide
- Name in the active language mode, the public photo with its credit, loading and unavailable states.

## Do
- Respect reduced motion and the school mode.

## Don't
- Don't generate or invent food photos.
