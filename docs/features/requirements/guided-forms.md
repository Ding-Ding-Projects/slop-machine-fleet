# Guided forms

Forms assume the person does not know what to type: pickers use real data, valid suggested defaults replace blank boxes, validation is inline, browse and typed paths share validation, and expert settings retain honest novice controls with a Custom state. Every capable interactive element offers an optional target-bound guided wizard directly above it, with one contextual step for simple controls and full steps for complicated workflows, with the direct route usable through identical validation, permissions, super-confirmation, action and history; contextual steps provide Back, Cancel, recovery and truthful results. Every element that can support guidance has complete unique contextual explanation and clear documentation covering purpose, options, defaults, effects, examples, validation and recovery, with stable authoritative concept/help identifiers, localized search/palette navigation and no repeated generic paragraphs or automatically reopening guidance. Hand-written per-element guidance and control-complexity inventories record capability reasons for genuine nonapplicability and negative regressions catch omitted wizards, missing coverage and repetition. Every editable textbox has a dedicated clear control with localized keyboard access and its intended validation/persistence, while noneditable fields and secrets remain protected.
