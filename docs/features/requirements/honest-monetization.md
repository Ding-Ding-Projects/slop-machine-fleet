# Free by default, honest when paid, never nagging

A application may be monetized only by its owner's deliberate decision, and free stays the default for every new one. Accessibility, the three language modes and both funny levels, School mode and the unlock ladder, and export and import of a person's own data never sit behind a payment; nothing lapses, a purchase surface states the exact price, inventory, and every licensing limitation, and payment credentials and signing keys follow the ordinary secret rules. No surface nags with unsolicited prompts for payments, donations, reviews, upgrades, or support, and donations for a application built on someone else's work point at them.

## Applicability

Applicable product surfaces must implement this behavior individually. A feature on one surface does not establish delivery on another.

## Current delivery state

## Acceptance

Verify the real behavior, localization, accessibility, persistence and reset where state exists, failure and recovery paths, and genuine built-surface evidence. Source presence alone is insufficient.
