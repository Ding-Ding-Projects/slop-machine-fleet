# Drawing and annotation with Drawesome

Every application lets a person draw on screenshots, frames, previews, images or a blank board with Drawesome's toolset: seven pens with their own width behaviour, a partial Eraser, Shift-locked strokes, and one shape-changing toolbar that turns into a palette, sliders or a minimised disc. Every pen has a working shortcut shown in its tooltip and menu; undo, redo, a guarded clear, SVG export of exactly what is shown and PNG export at 1x to 3x are required. Strokes stay local, persist and join version history; the chrome is genuine Material Design 3 and takes per-element appearance edits.

Applicable product surfaces must implement this behavior individually. A feature on one surface does not establish delivery on another.
